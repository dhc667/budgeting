# Space members have equal rights, backed by an audit trail rather than roles

Spaces are for families, so there are no owner, editor or viewer roles: any member can add any user in the instance, remove members, and create, edit or delete anything in the space. Instead of permissions, every change to a space's data is recorded with who made it and what it was before. The only structural rule is that a space can't lose its last member.

Transactions can be edited and deleted freely rather than corrected with reversing entries, as strict bookkeeping would require. The activity record keeps the previous version, which gives the same guarantee that nothing is silently lost.

The activity record covers everything a space owns (transactions, accounts, categories, budgets, recurring transactions) and membership changes. A member who leaves keeps credit for their past transactions in the space.
