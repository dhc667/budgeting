# An expense or income uses an account and a category from the same space

Only transfers may cross spaces, for example topping up a joint account from a private one. A family purchase paid with a private card is an expense in the payer's private space, not in the family's. Letting it land in the family's categories would amount to recording who paid for whom, which is bill-splitting, and that is out of scope (see the glossary's definition of a shared account). If the family should bear a cost, the money comes out of the shared account.
